Mono

Track spending. Nothing more.

Mono is a minimalist personal finance tracker for iPhone. Create spending categories called blocks, log your expenses in a couple of seconds, and keep an eye on your month with Home Screen widgets. Everything stays on your device — no account, no sign-up.

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Mono and choose small, medium or large. Tap the widget any time to open Mono, ready to add an expense.

In Mono, open Trends and tap the gear to open Settings, then Widget. Small and medium widgets show all your blocks or one block you pick; the large widget shows two or three blocks. You can also choose the currency for the total.

To protect your privacy, Mono hides amounts and block names in the widget while your iPhone is locked. They appear again as soon as you unlock it.

Open Settings and tap Erase All Data. This permanently deletes every block, expense and backup copy on this iPhone. Your settings stay. It can’t be undone. Your iCloud Backup is kept; delete it in the iOS Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Mono.

Mono uses fixed, approximate exchange rates built into the app, so it works fully offline. You can set your own rates in Settings → Currencies, or enter a rate when you change a block’s currency.

Intent

Plan the trip. Keep the journey.

Intent is a trip planner for iPhone. Every trip is a travel note: the cities you’ll visit, places and notes for each day, and every ticket and booking in one place. Finished trips fill in your world map. Everything stays on your device — no account, no sign-up.

Frequently asked questions

On My Trips, tap + and choose the country and dates. Open the trip and tap Add Step to add the cities you’ll visit. In a city, tap Add for places and notes: give them a day and a time, or leave them in Not planned yet for later.

Open the trip’s Documents tab and tap Add File, or add files to a city, a place or the journey between cities. Flight tickets show as boarding-pass cards with the route and flight number, and everything opens in the app, even offline.

When a trip ends it moves to the Archive and its countries fill in your map. Archived trips can be looked through, shared or deleted, and you can still change their cover photo, but nothing else is edited so your memories stay as they were.

Open the Map, tap the country and choose Mark Visited. For small countries that are hard to tap, use the search button next to the tab bar to find a country and jump to it.

On the Map, tap Compare Maps. Show your map code, or tap Scan a Friend’s Code to scan theirs. You can also send your map as a file. A map code holds only a name, your countries and your city count.

Open the trip and tap the share button. Your friend opens the file in Intent and gets their own copy, with its cities, places, notes and files.
